## Sources and reporting limits

- Ten formerly missing AUM funds now have automated official report extraction: Abakkus, Bank of India, Franklin, ICICI Prudential, JM, PGIM, Sundaram, The Wealth Company, TRUST and UTI. Their documents or raw official responses are archived with hashes.
- Baroda BNP Paribas, Union, Groww and LIC have reviewed figures from publicly readable AMC reports. Automatic downloads currently fail here. Their reporting dates, original links, review notes and lack of downloaded originals remain explicit; a reviewed observation is not a successful automated collection. Baroda also has a monthly digital-report parser ready for successful transfers.
- Groww uses its older April 2026 report. LIC uses its May report and the stated default last-working-day basis (29 May), rather than substituting the review date.
- Bandhan remains the one AUM gap: the accessible third-party figure has no verifiable reporting date, and the official interactive report could not be retrieved. No number was assigned an invented date.
- Axis labels its dated value only “Expense Ratio”; it is stored separately from TER/BER. Mahindra now supplies separate Direct/Regular BER. HSBC plan-specific fees are visible in a named-plan table; its ambiguous NAV feed is not silently assigned to Direct or Regular.
- Complete portfolios reconcile the reported stock, debt, repo and cash positions against the disclosed total. HDFC, Franklin, Helios, Motilal Oswal, Nippon, Quantum and The Wealth Company have complete snapshots. Existing partial snapshots and their IDs are preserved.
- UTI’s consolidated monthly ZIP is archived and its exact Small Cap section is extracted. Its current disclosure abbreviates some small weights and deposit details, so its holdings stay partial. Sundaram has a similar small-weight limitation.
- Official source discovery follows public monthly lists, digital factsheets and document directories. Every nightly run retains existing valid observations when a page is blocked, missing or unrecognized. Historical data starts with the reports actually obtained; intervening periods are not fabricated.
- The SBI/Franklin document ownership correction remains in force. Communications remain AMC-only.
